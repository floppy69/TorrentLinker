Your profile stays up to date.

Click Connect companion in TorrentLinker once. This browser keeps the connection across restarts and updates your profile every two minutes while a signed-in c411 tab is open.

What is synced

Account identity, Zen balance, class, level, transfer totals, total seed time, validated upload count and active seed count. When you import or enable Community, active seed titles/hashes, writable general/class channels and action counters are also sent to your connected TorrentLinker app. Cookies, CSRF tokens and API keys stay in the browser.

Sign in to c411 with the account shown in TorrentLinker. If your session expires or the account changes, updates wait until that account is signed in again.

Actions you schedule

Manage comments, votes and general/class chat in Zen → Community. Only drafts you explicitly queue can be sent, while the queue is enabled. The app controls timers and daily limits. Pausing this companion pauses profile updates, descriptor downloads and community actions.

Posting receipts are retained until the app acknowledges them, including across browser restarts and reconnects. An uncertain send is never repeated automatically. No media uploads are performed.

Share a Community preset

Choose an exported RSS, ReleaseScore or prompt JSON. The linked app removes private data and returns a preview. Only your confirmed preview is sent to torrentlinker.fr.

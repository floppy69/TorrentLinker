# Zen rules checked for issue #72

Checked on 2026-11-17 through the authenticated browser, starting from the [wiki](https://c411.org/wiki), then the [official Zen guide](https://c411.org/guide/tokens), the class scoring explanation and the advanced calculator in [Mes Zen](https://c411.org/user/zens). No tracker settings or payouts were changed.

Confirmed: decimal GB; size / 4 GB up to 4 GB; minimum weight 0.0001; published examples 1.26 at 25 GB and 1.40 at 60 GB; no seed contribution below 30 minutes; prorating by the fraction of the day shared. Upload classes count an upload independently of size. Previously paid days retain their recorded weight.

The guide's Tanker example gives approximately four Zen for fifteen 4 GB films with ninety seed days, note 1.7 and a 5% bonus. Public estimator tests now exercise that example, its half-day result and the eligibility threshold.

## Unresolved details

- The large-torrent curve is not published. The existing 1/8 exponent remains an approximation. The calculator's 1.64 upper limit does not establish an exact formula.
- Issue #72 reports truncating each torrent's weight multiplied by its day share, including zero for a minimum-weight torrent shared half a day. The reviewed pages do not specify the precision or order of that operation. No truncation precision has been invented in code.
- The guide describes rounding separately by bonus group; the estimator assumes one common-bonus group. It does not model the self-upload exemption shown by Mes Zen.

The bonus-group wording conflicts with [ADR-0005](../adr/0005-own-catalogue-and-zen-rules.md), whose October revision treats the total-day formula as superseding group rounding. The November guide does not support that supersession. This is an explicit evidence conflict, not a change to the estimator's grouping model.

The confirmed size correction already shipped. Keep #72 open for the remaining precision/formula evidence. These checks do not establish an exact match to every nightly payout, and historical recorded payouts must not be recalculated.
